CASEFLOW

Immigration Workflow Automation Demonstration

CaseFlow is a proof-of-concept demonstrating how document-heavy case workflows could be organized into a structured process for review.

The demonstration focuses on identifying information across multiple documents, comparing that information, and directing potential discrepancies to a human reviewer.

Start the Demonstration

HOW IT WORKS

Demonstration Workflow

1

Review Documents

Examine a set of fictional documents associated with a demonstration case.

2

Extract Information

Identify relevant information from the documents and keep track of where each piece of information came from.

3

Compare Information

Compare information appearing in different documents and identify potential discrepancies or variations.

4

Review Findings

Present potential issues to a human reviewer rather than automatically making legal decisions.

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Technical Concepts

Workflow Automation

Turning a sequence of repetitive document-review activities into a structured workflow.

Structured Information

Representing information in a form that can be compared, validated, and used by other parts of an application.

AI-Assisted Review

Using AI as one component of a larger workflow while keeping a human reviewer responsible for decisions.

Source Tracking

Keeping track of which document provided information so that findings can be reviewed against their source.

LIMITATIONS

What This POC Does Not Do

This site is a proof of concept, not a finished application.

The demonstration is intentionally limited so that the underlying workflow can be shown without introducing the infrastructure and security requirements of a production legal application.

  • The demonstration uses fictional information only.
  • It does not accept real client documents.
  • Users cannot upload documents to the site.
  • Users cannot download documents from the site.
  • The demonstration does not maintain real client records or case files.
  • AI-assisted analysis is intended to support human review.
  • The system does not provide legal advice.
  • The system does not determine immigration eligibility.

DEMO DATA

About the Demonstration Case

The demonstration uses a fictional case and fictional supporting records. The records are designed to contain information that can be compared across documents, including dates, names, addresses, and other case information.

The purpose is to demonstrate how a system could identify information that deserves human attention. It is not intended to reproduce official government documents or make conclusions about an actual person's immigration case.
