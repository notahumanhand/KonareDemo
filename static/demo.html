Proof-of-Concept Limitations

This is a software demonstration, not a production legal application.

  • All information used by this demonstration is fictional.
  • The demonstration does not accept or store real client documents.
  • Documents cannot be uploaded to or downloaded from this demonstration.
  • AI-assisted findings are intended to support human review, not replace it.
  • The system does not provide legal advice or determine immigration eligibility.
  • This prototype does not represent a production-ready document management, case management, or legal services system.
